<objective>
Complete D-02's "every ingress" for MINT-03: the sync-admission gate. A v1 mint arriving through CRDT sync is admitted only once its slot certificate exists locally (certificate-conditional Stall — never a blanket type block), deferring on the D-03 bounded window and rejecting on expiry. This is the orchestrator's selected shape: it satisfies D-02's every-ingress wording, D-03's verdict model, and keeps the migration's zero-value fillers and historical sync working.

Purpose: FilterTransaction (TransactionManager.cpp:3772-3832) admits any signature-valid transaction; it cannot synchronously distinguish historical (certified) from new (uncertified) v1 mints because deltas arrive unordered. Since 21-05 blocks new v1 mints from ever obtaining a certificate, "certificate exists" exactly separates history from new submissions: history converges to admitted, new attacks converge to deadline rejection.

Output: Classified filter verdict with the certificate-conditional branch, config-wired defer window, stall-resolution/deadline/migration/certified compat tests.
</objective>
