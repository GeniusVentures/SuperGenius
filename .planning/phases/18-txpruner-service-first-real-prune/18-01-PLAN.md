<objective>
Make pruned roots terminal at the single broadcast intake (PRUNE-05, decision D-02).

Purpose: after Phase-18's first real erase, the block backing a pruned root is gone, so today's `HasBlock`-based re-delivery dedup evaporates and `EnqueueRootCID` would re-fetch the deleted root onto the single DAG worker (the fetch-resurrect loop the milestone research flags as the top pitfall). The never-erased seal markers are the dedup of record: after a `HasBlock` miss, `isResolved(cid) == true` means processed-and-pruned — skip.

Output: the one-insertion D-02 guard in `HandleCIDBroadcast`, a test-observable fetch-attempt counter on `EnqueueRootCID`, and the SC-3 two-node proof (zero fetch attempts for re-delivered pruned roots, both delivery modes, with a negative control).
</objective>
