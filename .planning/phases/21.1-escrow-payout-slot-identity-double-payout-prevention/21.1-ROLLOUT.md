## Ordered inventory

Perform the following for **every participating validator and every relevant store**, including offline/restarting validators, payout submitters and passive replicas. Use operator-provided consistent read-only copies and existing owning C++ read APIs. There is no comprehensive inventory dashboard or CLI in this repository. Opening a normal node against a copy can run recovery and change it; retain the immutable original copy and use read-only analysis. Record read/decode/authorization failures explicitly.

1. **Establish the complete roster and preservation point.** Record node/account/peer identity, role, network ID and private-network scope, configured database paths, source revision, build identity, executable digest, configuration digest and copy time/digest. Preserve all databases, raw CRDT blocks and canonical history before any operational change. Include each node's local account/nonce and UTXO stores as well as its GlobalDB datastore; one replica's copy cannot stand in for another validator's private state. Capture any available volatile owner diagnostics before shutdown; a disk copy cannot recover every in-memory queue.
2. **Record registry authority.** Use `ValidatorRegistry::LoadCurrentRegistry()`, `GetRegistryCid()`, `GetRegistryEpoch()` and `LoadRegistryUpdate()` (`src/blockchain/ValidatorRegistry.hpp:269,317,481,486`). Export the serialized update, full membership IDs, active/genesis flags, weights, epoch and CID. Validate referenced authority through `LoadVerifiedRegistryByCid()` (`:294`) and preserve its genesis-rooted update ancestry and configured authority identity. The CRDT object key is `gnus-validator-registry`; the raw CID key is `gnus-validator-registry-cid`. Use the instance's `RegistryKeyValue()` / `RegistryCidKeyValue()` for private scopes, which append `/<network_scope>` (`:546-595`). An unverified registry loader or a locally configured weight list does not establish authorization.
3. **Export exact legacy payments and markers.** Enumerate each monitored network's `/bc-<network-id>/tx/` values through `GlobalDB::QueryKeyValues()` (`src/crdt/globaldb/globaldb.hpp:150`) and decode through `TransactionManager::FetchTransaction()` / `DeSerializeTransaction()` (`src/transaction/TransactionManager.hpp:365-369`). Use `GetTransactionPath()` (`:357-359`), never infer a hash from a filename alone. Export original signed bytes, decoded type, intrinsic hash, source, nonce, slot, chain ID, all inputs and outputs, and any job/hold linkage. `GeniusTransaction::SerializeByteVector()`, `GetHash()`, `GetSrcAddress()`, `GetNonce()` and `GetSlotID()` own these values (`src/account/GeniusTransaction.hpp`). Old `transfer` payments retain their `source:nonce` slot (`:290`). Legacy `escrow-release` markers retain original hold hash, release amount/address and their original bytes (`src/account/EscrowReleaseTransaction.hpp:35-41`); they are status history, not a payment receipt. Use `TransactionManager::LookupTransaction()` (`:410`) to distinguish missing from pruned history, and preserve `/prune/tx/<hash>` evidence and available archived bytes. Unrecoverable signed history remains a gap.
4. **Export and validate canonical certificates.** Inventory CRDT `/cert/<canonical-slot>` records and preserve their exact serialized bytes, proposal, embedded transaction, vote identities/signatures, registry CID/epoch and validation verdict. Use `ConsensusManager::GetExpectedCertificateSlotKey()`, `GetCertificateBySlot()` and, when needed, `FindCertificateByNonceSubject()` (`src/blockchain/Consensus.hpp:640,653,662`). Decode the nonce subject using `DecodeNonceSubject()` and the owning embedded transaction decoder. Check exact hash/source/nonce/slot and embedded signed bytes, following the private `TransactionManager::CertificateMatchesTransaction()` / `GetCertifiedApplicationEvidence()` contract (`src/transaction/TransactionManager.cpp:1915-1972`). A certificate for a shared slot does not certify a different contender. Do not treat a hash index, tracked `CONFIRMED` status or unavailable validation dependency as canonical finality. Preserve rejected and deferred records with their verdicts too.
5. **Reconcile every hold with ledger evidence.** Export the hold transaction and its output-zero outpoint, owner, token and amount; list every linked legacy/V2 payment and status marker. Read `UTXOManager::GetOutPointSpender()`, `IsOutPointGenuinelyConsumed()` and `ReadDurableSpendEvidence()` (`src/account/UTXOManager.hpp:453,469,499`). There is no separate `holdspender` namespace. The durable record is `/utxo/<owner>/<transaction-hash>:<output-index>` (`src/account/UTXOManager.cpp:26`); `UTXOEntryRecord` contains state, amount/token, creation/spend epochs and `spent_by_txid` (`:1285-1307`). Pair spender identity with genuine consumption: a synthesized zero-amount tombstone may also name a spender. Export every recipient output and per-token balance using `GetUTXOs(address)`, `GetAllUTXOs()` and `GetBalance(token,address)` (`src/account/UTXOManager.hpp:233,263,156`), including raw records and `/utxo-checkpoint/<owner>/<epoch>`. Compare all replicas, preserving differences rather than choosing a convenient local view.
6. **Preserve private vote commitments.** Enumerate raw local RocksDB `/consensus/vote/<slot>` and `/consensus/equivocation/<slot>/<voter>` through the datastore's read-only query API (`src/blockchain/Consensus.hpp:755-758`; `GlobalDB::GetDataStore()`, `src/crdt/globaldb/globaldb.hpp:194`). Decode `ActiveVoteRecord`: canonical slot, exact proposal bytes, exact vote bytes and acceptance deadline (`src/blockchain/impl/proto/Consensus.proto:156`). Include expired, malformed and unmatched records in the evidence with their errors. These records are private local durability state, not replicated pubsub messages. Never remove them to permit a replacement payout.
7. **Inventory pending and retry work.** Public read-only `GlobalDB::GetWorkJournal()` leads to `CRDTWorkJournal::ListUnfinished()` / `GetEntry()` (`src/crdt/globaldb/globaldb.hpp:293`; `src/crdt/globaldb/crdt_work_journal.hpp:98,105`). Export `/crdt/work/` records for logical `/cert/` keys with state, attempt count, updated time and lease deadline (`:50-57,117`). Do not call recovery/marking methods during inventory. `ConsensusManager::pending_entries_`, dependency indexes, pending votes and slot candidates are private in-memory state (`src/blockchain/Consensus.hpp:1307-1330`); the transaction manager's `pending_proposals_`, `tx_queue_m` and `tx_processed_m` are also private (`src/transaction/TransactionManager.hpp:832-838`). No public complete snapshot API exists. Record available existing owner diagnostics, exact proposal/vote/transaction identities and counts; mark inaccessible or lost volatile state as missing evidence, never as an empty queue.
8. **Inventory queued intents and unfinished jobs/results.** Use the task queue's `ListTaskKeys()`, `GetTask()`, `GetSubTasks()`, `GetTaskResult()` and `IsTaskCompleted()` contracts (`src/processing/impl/TaskQueueImpl.hpp:40-61`). `TaskKeys` owns `/processing_<version>/tasks/<id>`, `/subtasks/<task>/<subtask>`, `/claimable/<id>`, `/task_results/tasks<id>` and `/lock_<full-task-key>`; obtain exact paths from its builders, including the result path's existing lack of a slash before `<id>` (`src/processing/impl/TaskKeys.hpp:93-201`). Private scopes prepend `/chain/<network-id>`. `SubTaskResultStorage::GetSubTaskResults()` (`src/processing/processing_subtask_result_storage.hpp:29`) reads retained `results/<subtask-id>` records, with the same scope prefix when private. Export task bytes, escrow path, subtask completeness, validated result bytes and payout metadata, locks, claimability/completion state, and any queued signed payment/nonce. `CompleteTask()` stages its result and claimable removal in the atomic transaction passed to payout (`src/processing/impl/TaskQueueImpl.cpp:192-208`; `src/account/GeniusNode.cpp:4229-4260`); a queued completion may not yet be durable. Preserve retained results and explicitly identify missing volatile intents.

The exports are review evidence. They do not become a new authoritative settlement registry, activation allowlist or retrospective certificate fence.

## Disposition and coordinated admission

Before a separately authorized rollout, operators coordinate a payout-submission pause across all submitters while retaining ordinary historical evidence and existing finality handling. Record the actual pause acknowledgement for every submitter; this document installs no node-wide pause flag. Repeat the inventory as needed to explain changes during collection. A pause or a drained local queue does not prove absence of a withheld certificate.

Classify **every carried-forward hold and local payout intent**, cross-referencing all validators' evidence:

| Observed state | Required disposition before V2 admission for that hold |
|---|---|
| Exact validated legacy payment certificate, applied or awaiting replay | Preserve bytes, canonical certificate, original slot and nonce history. Follow the existing exact-certificate historical application path; reconcile genuine spender and all recipient effects. Do not rebuild a second payment. |
| Genuine consumed hold with exact validated V2 payment | Preserve the certified winner and reconcile recipient effects once across replicas. A marker or balance alone is insufficient. |
| Known local uncertified intent with complete validated results | Establish eligibility under the accepted historical boundary first. Retire/release through the existing owning transaction lifecycle and reconstruct a fresh V2 payment from retained results. Record old and new hashes/nonces/slots and the owner action's outcome. |
| Outstanding vote, pending certificate/dependency, conflicting replicas, missing history/results, unknown intent or ambiguous spender | Keep unresolved and record the blocker. Never infer eligibility from expiry, a missing local certificate or a successful local duplicate rejection. |

Existing operations have limits: private `RollbackTransactions()` changes a batch to `FAILED` (`src/transaction/TransactionManager.cpp:2354`); failure handling releases matching local input reservations and reserved nonces (`:5994,6004`). `UTXOManager::RollbackUTXOs()` only releases matching `RESERVED` entries and preserves consumed entries (`src/account/UTXOManager.cpp:706`). `GeniusAccount::ReleaseNonce()` removes only a local pending reservation (`src/account/GeniusAccount.cpp:947`); preserve confirmed nonce/hash history via `GetLocalConfirmedNonce()` / `GetLocalConfirmedTxHash()` (`src/account/GeniusAccount.hpp:272,279`). There is no public operator rollback/requeue command: do not invoke private methods through a fabricated tool or edit raw stores. If existing owner flow cannot perform a required disposition, record it as unresolved for the checkpoint.

Public `EnqueueTransaction()` (`src/transaction/TransactionManager.hpp:504`) is an existing lifecycle primitive, not permission to resubmit old bytes. Rebuild only eligible local uncertified intents with `PayEscrow()` / `AsyncPayEscrow()` (`src/transaction/TransactionManager.cpp:1315,1407`) and the retained complete validated results/completion transaction. Those functions construct and sign a new V2 payment. Never rewrite signed old transactions/proposals into V2, clear votes/certificates, delete UTXO spender evidence, or reset nonce/history to force progress. Vote release remains consensus-owned; acceptance deadlines alone do not authorize a replacement (`src/blockchain/Consensus.cpp:1513,1629-1680`).

Before any real-value V2 admission, require fixed builds on **all participating validators**, record each deployed executable/source/configuration identity and authorized registry view, and reconcile each carried-forward hold/intent. Test A/B/C fixtures do not establish an external validator roster or reproduce unrecovered staging configuration. No validator weights or quorum policy are changed by this rollout.

## Historical-finality boundary and final checkpoint

V2 contenders for one escrow share its canonical escrow outpoint slot. A historical `transfer` certificate retains its different `source:nonce` slot. Certificate validation and quorum tallying have no current-time expiry; proposal TTL, vote retry deadlines, timestamps, a claimed replay boolean or a local submission drain cannot prove that a valid old certificate was never signed or withheld. Exact canonical binding establishes what was certified, not whether the operator has accepted its eligibility across the upgrade boundary. Existing spender/duplicate checks can prevent a second local ledger effect; that observation does not establish cross-era certificate exclusivity or network-wide convergence over unknown historical state.

The blocking operator checkpoint in plan 21.1-08 must record one of two outcomes:

1. Explicit operator acceptance of the documented historical boundary and its evidence, including every carried-forward escrow/intent disposition, remaining limitations and all-validator readiness; any subsequent deployment still needs its own authorization.
2. Activation remains pending because evidence or acceptance is unavailable.

A requirement for an authenticated retrospective fence needs a separate explicit protocol decision defining its authenticated source and compatibility semantics. This procedure supplies no new timestamp/epoch cutoff, registry, allowlist or quarantine policy. Until that checkpoint is resolved, keep `activation_status` as `pending` and `operator_decision` as `null` in the evidence record.
