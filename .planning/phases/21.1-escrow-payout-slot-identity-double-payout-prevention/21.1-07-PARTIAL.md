Iteration 2 passed. No conflicting canonical certificate or second payment was reported. Do not infer that all ledger effects converged from the passing last-iteration XML.

The exact journal state at shutdown/reopen was not captured. Source inspection identifies two existing ownership hypotheses to distinguish: a committed certificate key can remain `Processing` under its three-minute callback lease, or a snapshotted weak-owner callback can return without applying while generic callback completion retires the journal entry. `ValidateCertificateBestProposal` rejection occurs only on pubsub arbitration cleanup and does not explain durable journal application by itself. These are hypotheses, not a proven production diagnosis. The next executor must capture the existing work-journal entry and per-node spender/output snapshots at the failing boundary, then fix the owning path narrowly; no store, fence, timeout, retry loop or runner policy should be invented.
