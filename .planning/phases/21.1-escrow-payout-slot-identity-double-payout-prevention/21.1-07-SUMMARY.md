## Recovery Investigation and Causality Limit

After the initial passing gates and metadata closeout, an additional execution exposed E's partial-restart topology race. The logs show crossed Noise handshake failures between reused listener tuples. Replacing E's partial restart with the existing `RestartAndReconnect` helper preserves the full lifecycle and removes surviving old hosts before the explicit mesh barrier; it did not establish a passing recovery gate.

In the two-repeat focused run, iteration 1 retained a valid canonical payout certificate on every peer but one peer lacked the complete expected payment after the 25s catch-up condition and duplicate certificate submission. Iteration 2 passed. No conflicting canonical certificate or second payment was reported. Do not infer that all ledger effects converged from the passing last-iteration XML.

The historical failed run did not capture exact journal state at shutdown/reopen. The continuation added per-peer journal state, attempts, lease and timestamps before restart, after Consensus close, before reopened-manager creation and after catch-up, alongside genuine hold spender, every output and recipient balance. Both pre-fix diagnostic repetitions passed; their E entries were Stalled or absent at reopen and replayed normally. The three-minute Processing lease remains a source-level possibility and was not changed without evidence.

The deterministic regression instead establishes a precise loss mechanism: CRDT snapshots the actual Consensus callback, a preceding overlapping callback blocks delivery, the old Consensus owner is closed and destroyed, and delivery resumes. The outer weak callback silently returned, allowing generic completion to remove Processing work. After the signed write committed, the journal was absent and a replacement manager received zero handler calls. The fix captures the existing journal and uses MarkSeen plus MarkStalled(0) in that expired-owner branch. This preserves retry scheduling while the existing durable readback, canonical validation and transaction handler retain all application authority. The final completed snapshot retains Stalled work; replacement dispatch applies exactly once and removes it.

The original intermittent E failure is not proven to have this as its sole cause: no failed-run boundary snapshot exists, and instrumented pre-fix E passed. The proven callback-loss defect has a RED/GREEN regression, and the required final repeated and complete local network gates pass. This is the technical basis for completing plan 07, with the historical causality limit preserved. No lease, TransactionManager application rule, store, fence, timeout, retry loop or runner policy was changed. The independent source review found no ownership cycle or new shutdown blocker.

Final callback/application and compatibility checks passed on the same tested source: 42 Consensus lifecycle/contract cases, 45 exact-certificate application cases, one smoke case and the process-ownership check. The saved five-case xUnit reports five enabled cases and no failures/errors.
