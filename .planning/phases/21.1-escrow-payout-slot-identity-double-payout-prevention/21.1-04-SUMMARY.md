## Accomplishments

- Registered the dedicated legacy tag-7 status codec and V2 tag-10 payment codec in raw and embedded manager dispatch. Legacy marker apply/revert retains its status/topic meaning without consuming inputs, producing UTXOs or establishing payment completion. Fresh marker proposals remain rejected.
- Replaced the payment Transfer emitted by PayEscrow with one signed EscrowReleaseTransactionV2. Existing BuildPayoutOutputs supplies all peer, developer and burn outputs. Existing all-results gating, processing assignment, one-peer locks and takeover behavior remain in their owning code; no new processor allowlist or settlement store was introduced.
- Applied V2 effects using the certified actual hold owner and existing payment helpers. A different spender is rejected before credit, own-spender replay preserves the genuine input record, and rollback requires durable genuine spend evidence naming this payment. Unapplied losers cannot restore the winner's hold or delete its outputs. Completion requires the actual confirmed payment spender and its effects.
- Added private copy-only CertifiedApplicationEvidence with immutable manager identity, slot and signed raw bytes. Construction requires the owning validated GetCertificateBySlot lookup, CertificateMatchesTransaction, exact decoded transaction bytes, and equality of the original embedded protobuf against the transaction's embedded serialization. This also rejects unknown protobuf fields that decoding would otherwise discard.
- Carried evidence explicitly through FetchAndProcessTransaction, AddTransactionToProcessedMaps and both OnConsensusCertificate branches into ChangeTransactionState and ParseTransaction. The parser rechecks the immutable evidence. Ordinary callers receive no historical authority, and no public replay flag or timestamp rule was added.
- Historical Transfer ownership is resolved from the actual input at hold output zero and the exact certified EscrowTransaction. Uncle text only selects compatibility inspection; it cannot supply an owner. Ordinary Transfer application uses source ownership and rejects known foreign input owners before the shared ledger helper can mutate state. Historical reversal uses the real hold owner and genuine own-spender evidence.
- Closed plan 03's deferred full V2 nonce-ingress coverage: actual HandleNonceConsensusSubject approve, invalid-payment reject and missing-hold/certificate pending paths are included in the fast target.

## Decisions Made

- Ordinary/mint callback behavior may proceed without historical evidence when no canonical record is available; this confers no escrow-owner exception. Historical Transfer payment requires the private evidence-bearing path.
- Exact embedded bytes are checked before any historical ownership exception; a matching hash/slot alone does not cover excluded signatures or protobuf fields discarded during decoding.
- Evidence proves binding to a validated canonical record. It does not prove that the record predates activation, establish an exhaustive historical inventory or prevent withheld old certificates across eras.
