- Every implementation task has an automated gate; no three consecutive tasks can omit verification.
- Fast codec/shape/local-validation CTest commands have explicit 30/50-second timeout bounds. Their actual latency is unmeasured until execution. A timeout is a failed/unresolved check, never evidence of completion.
- Existing integration filters are candidates for fast feedback; measure them before claiming sub-minute latency. Complete fixture targets, network/restart battery, builds, format/tidy and full suite are separate measured gates.
- Report actual selected case counts, nonzero assertions, pass/failure/timing and unavailable checks. Never rely on an unregistered target or a vacuous GTest filter.
- Use real signed distinct accounts and the real persisted UTXO fixtures, bounded waitForCondition and fresh independent stores. Do not replace ledger assertions with status markers or fake balance counters.

## Manual-only activation gate

**21.1-08-2 is a blocking-human decision, not an automated approval.** Required operator evidence: every coordinated validator's build and authorized registry CID/epoch/membership; accepted exact old payment/canonical certificate history; every escrow hold/outpoint/spender/balance; durable active votes; pending proposals/certificate retries; queued payout intents; unfinished jobs/complete validated results; each carried-forward escrow's disposition; and the explicit accepted historical-finality boundary.

A local drain, old timestamp, replay boolean, or elapsed proposal/vote TTL cannot prove a withheld old quorum certificate does not exist. Old transfer certificates remain in old source:nonce slots; no authenticated activation fence was found. Fixture tests cannot prove completeness of unrecovered deployed history. Preserve exact-certificate-bound historical application while fresh Transfer admission rejects the removed bypass.

Permitted dispositions are explicit boundary acceptance with actual evidence for a later separately authorized all-validator activation, activation remains pending, or a request for a separately authorized authenticated-boundary protocol decision. Never invent operator acceptance, reset stores, clear finality/votes, introduce a per-escrow finality registry or claim automatic cross-era exclusivity. No deployment is authorized.
