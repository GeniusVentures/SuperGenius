direct GossipPubSubTopic subscriptions in SGNUS code (not the per-subtask libp2p host, which
materializes only an io_context today and receives nothing):
1. Grid channel — `ProcessingServiceImpl::Listen` subscribes
   (processing_service.cpp:126-139) → `OnMessage` (:165+)

   (processing_subtask_queue_accessor_impl.cpp:112-119) → `OnResultChannelMessage`

   (processing_subtask_queue_channel_pubsub.cpp:20-28) → `OnProcessingChannelMessage`

These carry private job coordination data (grid requests/responses, subtask results, queue
ownership/sync) on the scoped channels from 15-06 — exactly the "message-handling points" the
owner direction (deferred-items.md §3) designates. Each gets the same 15-11 gate at entry: when a
membership filter is installed, a message whose transport `from` peer is not an authorized member
(unknown/malformed from included) is dropped before any handling. No filter → pass-through
(public unchanged). No vendored trees, no gater allow-list — pure SGNUS application layer.

Wiring: GeniusNode passes `MakeNetworkMembershipFilter(network_registry_)` into
`ProcessingServiceImpl::SetMembershipFilter` (mirroring the setBitswap propagation pattern at
processing_service.cpp:112-124: store + apply to all existing m_processingNodes + apply at node
creation), and ProcessingNode forwards to its SubTaskQueueAccessorImpl (results channel) and its
ProcessingSubTaskQueueChannelPubSub (queue channel) — both held at processing_node.cpp:136-185.

Purpose: unauthorized same-PSK peers cannot participate in private job processing even though
they can mesh. Output: three gated handlers + wiring + tests.
