## Destruction-reentrancy hazard (design constraint)

The element-filter lambda holds a strong shared_ptr<NetworkRegistry> (entry.peer_registry). Removing
the filter destroys that capture; if it is the LAST reference, ~NetworkRegistry re-enters
Unregister(). Therefore Unregister must order: (1) UnregisterIf the policy entry, (2) stop/join the
refresh thread, (3) UnregisterNewElementCallback, (4) UnregisterFiltersFor LAST — and
UnregisterFiltersFor on an already-removed pattern must be a safe no-op (verify
GlobalDB/CRDTDataStore::UnregisterElementFilter tolerates a missing pattern). No entry destruction
may happen under the registry mutex (mirror the extract-then-destroy pattern).
</context>
