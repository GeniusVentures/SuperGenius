<objective>
Make job scope explicit and propagate it through every job-derived artifact (D-08) — at the REAL key-construction sites, not just helper level: CRDT task keys (all 15 TaskKeys:: sites live in TaskQueueImpl.cpp, not GeniusNode.cpp), subtask result keys (SubTaskResultStorageImpl's literal results/ prefix), the escrow CRDT path (ProcessImage → CreateEscrowInfoCRDTTransaction), the processing channel topic (AddListenTopic alignment), the job grid channel (DHT CID derivation in DHTInit and the StartProcessing subscription), and the escrow chain id (per-tx GetChainId override). Public scope keeps today's byte-identical strings; private scope lands under `/chain/<privateNetworkId>/`.

Purpose: Without scoped keys/topics/chain-ids at the data-path sites, a private network's job state would collide with (and leak into) the public network's replicated data. Helper-only overloads nobody calls would deliver nothing (checker finding, 2026-09-01).
Output: Scope-aware key builders consumed by TaskQueueImpl/SubTaskResultStorageImpl/GeniusNode escrow path, scoped channel alignment, escrow chain-id override, data-path isolation tests.
</objective>
