<objective>
Make job scope explicit and propagate it through every job-derived artifact (D-08): CRDT task keys, chain ids, and pubsub topics — public scope 0 keeps today's byte-identical strings, private scope lands under `/chain/<privateNetworkId>/`.

Purpose: Without scoped keys/topics/chain-ids, a private network's job state would collide with (and leak into) the public network's replicated data.
Output: Scope-aware TaskKeys/chain-id/topic helpers, GeniusNode propagation, isolation tests.
</objective>
