<interfaces>
`ConsensusManager::MapCertificateReadError(std::error_code)` maps NOT_FOUND to `CertificateStoreError::NotFound`, CORRUPTION to `IntegrityError`, and all other/unknown errors to `StorageError`.
`certificate_record_reader_` is the private friend-injectable read seam initialized to `db_->Get`.
Current `SubmitCertificate` and `FilterCertificateDelta` bypass that seam and call `db_->Get` directly, then inspect only `has_value()`.
</interfaces>
