<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Certificate delta → registry store | A valid certificate may arrive before the CID it needs for signature/quorum validation |
| Delta filter decision → DAG processing | Collapsing stall into rejection destroys data; merging before validation admits untrusted state |
| Retry queue → CRDT worker | Unbounded immediate retry can become a CPU/memory denial of service |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Severity | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-09-G08-01 | High | Tampering / Denial of Service | Stalled certificate pair | resolved in-plan | Do not merge or process CID until full validation approves |
| T-09-G08-02 | High | Denial of Service | Registry/certificate reordering | resolved in-plan | Preserve source job and retry without peer rebroadcast |
| T-09-G08-03 | Medium | Denial of Service | Retry scheduler | resolved in-plan | 256-global/32-per-dependency caps, 10-minute TTL, eight-attempt limit, deterministic admission rejection, complete cleanup, deduplication, observability, and shutdown drain |
| T-09-G08-04 | High | Tampering / Denial of Service | Remote certificate tombstones | resolved in-plan | Match delta filters on tombstones, terminally reject every `/cert/` tombstone, and remove it before `CrdtSet::Merge`; exact-ID replication/restart regressions |
| T-09-G08-05 | Medium | Denial of Service | Continuous ordinary-root arrivals | resolved in-plan | Due-time FIFO queue snapshot bounds precedence; post-snapshot arrivals wait and TTL cannot evict before the guaranteed evaluation |
</threat_model>
