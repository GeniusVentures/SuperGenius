The test sends N=3 ERC-1155 burn transactions to the local Anvil fork BEFORE starting any node, then bootstraps a 3-node GeniusNode cluster whose `bridge_chains_config.json` points the sepolia chain entry at the local Anvil RPC. The test verifies the recipient's balance increases by >= N × kMintAmount without any manual mint call in the test body.
