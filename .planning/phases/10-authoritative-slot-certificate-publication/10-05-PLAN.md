<interfaces>
`ValidatorRegistry::LoadCertificateBySubjectHash` currently concatenates `/cert/` with batch metadata and is called during batch creation and validation. `OnFinalizedCertificate` has the full certificate and can derive `ConsensusManager::GetSlotKey(certificate.proposal())` at intake. Preserve `subject_hashes` as the batch root/identity, but retain a companion in-memory subject-hash → generic-slot association for the existing pending batch window; lack of that association is unavailable, never permission to query `/cert/<subject-hash>`.
</interfaces>
