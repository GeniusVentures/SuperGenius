<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Risk |
|---|---|
| Candidate concurrency → signer | Deadline races could sign two proposals |
| Signed bytes → publication/recovery | Reconstruction could create a distinct usable signature |
| Local timeout → lock retirement | Early retirement could enable equivocation while the old vote is acceptable |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Severity | Category | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-10-04-01 | High | Elevation of Privilege | One generation-tagged `SigningPublishing` reservation spans freeze, signing, durable put, and unlocked raw publication; `FinalizeSlot` waits for that exact reservation, while a pre-existing `Finalizing` reservation prevents any later vote work |
| T-10-04-02 | High | Tampering / Repudiation | Exact signed vote and envelope bytes commit before raw publish and are replayed without reconstruction |
| T-10-04-03 | High | Tampering | Retirement uses the exact first-acceptance predicate and commits before new eligibility |
| T-10-04-04 | Medium | Denial of Service | Owned timer and explicit deadline processing avoid detached callbacks and unbounded extension |
</threat_model>
