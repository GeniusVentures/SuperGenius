## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Canonical confirmed record to local RocksDB | Signed trust evidence becomes the restart high-water mark. |
| Concurrent candidate callbacks to durable head | Multiple valid successors race for one predecessor. |

| T13-07 | Tampering | restart load | mitigate | Persisted LKG remains authoritative; missing/older/fork state never clears it. | rollback/missing/fork cases |
| T13-11 | Tampering/Rollback | `TrustStateStore` | mitigate | Synchronous RocksDB batch, canonical record hashes/proofs, load-and-verify, and failure injection. | `trust_state_store_test` |
