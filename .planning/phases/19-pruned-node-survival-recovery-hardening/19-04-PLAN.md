<objective>
Close GATE-05's checkpoint-rebuild half (D-01): on checkpoint mismatch, the clear-and-rebuild path uses the 19-01 preserve-clear, the replay skips missing/pruned refs as not relevant (marker consult at the missing-set insertion), the double-spend check recognizes the spender's OWN rebuild replay (the derived hazard research uncovered), and every gap-filled READY UTXO is cross-checked against durable consumed-outpoint records — the double-spend window closes by construction, never by refusing to boot (refuse-and-resync was rejected per D-01).

Purpose: This is the phase's security core. With CONSUMED entries surviving the clear, PutUTXO's existing duplicate check (UTXOManager.cpp:166-172) refuses re-creation of spent outpoints; without the own-replay fix, preserved evidence would falsely reject the legitimate spender's replay and lose its change outputs (Pitfall 1).
Output: Four coordinated TM edits + the RebuildGuard suite family.
</objective>
