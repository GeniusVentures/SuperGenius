<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Risk |
|---|---|
| Semantic validation to active consensus candidate | Candidate can become vote-eligible before durable protection |
| Candidate-controlled fields to reservation identity | Proposers can split one burn into multiple local owners |
| Proposal cleanup to shared slot resource | Losing cleanup can unlock a still-live burn |

| T-11-04-04 | Medium | Denial of Service | Same-slot contenders idempotently join instead of being rejected by an existing local reservation |
