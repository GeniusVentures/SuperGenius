Add typed resource-finalization and exact-winner application disposition plumbing without changing certificate authority. In `FinalizeSlot`, after the authoritative certificate pair is established and the slot lifecycle is reserved, extract/cross-check the certified mint descriptor and durably create/transition its reservation to FinalizedPendingApplication with certificate digest, proposal ID, and winner transaction hash. Perform this before `PutPendingProcess`, handler lease/invocation, or cleanup. Re-entry with the same certificate is idempotent; a different binding is integrity/safety failure. If the reservation transition returns storage failure, return StorageFailure and execute zero handler/cleanup work. A node with no earlier proposal must synthesize the finalized record from the certified payload.

Replace ambiguous mint handler outcome mapping with a typed disposition: Applied/AlreadyApplied success, Retryable for storage/unavailable/missing-ready dependency, and Irreconcilable for exact different application, different winner, invalid consumed state, or reservation binding contradiction. Retryable leaves CertificateProcessing Pending and the exact FinalizedPendingApplication identity intact for automatic recovery/restart. Irreconcilable atomically writes SafetyError diagnostics tied to the certificate/winner, stops application retries, keeps the slot finalized, and suppresses cleanup that could reopen the burn. Existing normal subject handlers preserve their behavior.

Add deterministic barriers/counters proving durable final pending precedes handler entry and cleanup; injected transition failure invokes neither; certificate-only observation creates protection; transient failure restarts exact winner; irreconcilable state becomes SafetyError and stops retry; duplicate local/pubsub/CRDT/recovery paths share one handler lease and never replace the winner. Include finality racing proposal cleanup/admission: finality wins and state is never absent.
