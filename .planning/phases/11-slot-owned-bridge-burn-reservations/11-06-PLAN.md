Define one narrow `ConsensusStateStore::ApplyFinalizedReservationBatch`-style contract on the shared instance. The operation owns `ConsensusStateStore::mutex_` continuously across FinalizedPendingApplication reread, exact handle checks, participant staging, and eventual caller-owned batch commit. Document and enforce the total order `ConsensusStateStore::mutex_ -> UTXOManager::persistence_mutex_ -> UTXOManager::utxos_mutex_`. <threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Risk |
|---|---|
| Consensus finality to account application | Reconstructed or non-owning store references can bypass the live serialization authority |
| Store identity to UTXO persistence | Same-path independent datastore objects can create split-brain locking |
| Reservation gate to UTXO locks | Recursive or inverted acquisition can deadlock finality and application |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Severity | Category | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-11-06-01 | High | Tampering | Immutable handle carries the exact live shared store and exact finalized winner identity |
| T-11-06-02 | High | Spoofing | Shared-object datastore identity is required; filesystem-path equivalence is rejected |
| T-11-06-03 | High | Elevation of Privilege | One store gate covers every reservation writer and the complete participant boundary |
| T-11-06-04 | High | Denial of Service | Explicit store -> persistence -> state order, unlocked helpers, and no outer callbacks prevent recursive/inverted locking |
</threat_model>
