<objective>
Build the 11-node (1 Full + 10 Light) mint-race e2e fixture and the two core race tests: a
single contested burn and a 3-5 burn batch, proving the watcher-driven exactly-once mint
invariant holds under genuine cross-node concurrency (zero manual `MintTokens()` calls).

UTXO-reservation + CRDT-persistence dedup (traced in 08-RESEARCH.md) survives true concurrent
discovery by 11 independent watchers, not just sequential/staggered discovery.

build via `test/src/CMakeLists.txt`.
